Follow the logs:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward deploy/{{ $fullname }} {{ .Values.ports.health }} {{ .Values.ports.metrics }}
  curl -s localhost:{{ .Values.ports.health }}/ready
  curl -s localhost:{{ .Values.ports.metrics }}/metrics

sign when the bucket and the chain disagree. See the chart README before
